01 / Senior project
TECH460 · Academic design proposal · 2025

Smarter energy monitoring for GPU data centers

I developed a proposal for an IoT energy-monitoring system, using NVIDIA’s GPU data centers as the case-study setting. The design connects environmental and power sensors to analytics and cooling controls.

IoT architecture · Technology evaluation · Implementation planning · Validation strategy

01 / COLLECTEnvironmental & power sensors
02 / CONNECTIoT gateway
03 / ANALYZECloud & AI analytics
04 / ACTDashboard & cooling controls

Proposed system architecture, summarized from my final presentation.

Read project notes +

The challenge

Develop an approach to monitoring energy use and cooling conditions in GPU data centers, balancing efficiency, implementation cost, and operational disruption.

Design decisions

Compared IoT smart energy monitoring with AI-controlled liquid cooling. Recommended sensor-based monitoring for existing facilities because it could require fewer infrastructure changes; considered liquid cooling for future high-density facilities.

Implementation and validation

Created a work breakdown structure and phased schedule covering planning, design, implementation, testing, deployment, and staff training. Proposed a pilot in one data center zone, checks against manual energy audits, and user acceptance testing with facility managers.

Deliverable and scope

Completed a 24-slide final presentation with a problem statement, alternative analysis, architecture, rollout plan, and ongoing monitoring approach. This is an academic proposal with estimated benefits and a proposed validation process. It does not claim a deployed system, measured savings, or work performed for NVIDIA.
